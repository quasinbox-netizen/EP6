Receipts

Every other page is a backtest, written by someone who had already seen the outcome. This one is the exception: claims recorded before their window closed, scored when it closes.

In plain words. Every day this site writes down where it thinks the price will be within 10 and 30 days, and how sure it is. Nothing is edited afterwards. When the day arrives, the claim is marked right or wrong here - including the wrong ones.

Why a small count cannot be a grade
Each row is scored on a handful of settled claims so far. Five landings out of five is not a 100% method; it is five landings. The count is the column to read beside the result.
